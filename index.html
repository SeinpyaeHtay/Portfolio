About Me

I am a Civil Engineering professional with approximately 3 years of experience in construction drafting, architectural documentation and BIM modelling. I hold a Level 4 Diploma and Level 5 Advanced Diploma in Civil Engineering, with practical experience in both site engineering and technical drawing preparation.

I began my career as a Site Engineer cum Drafter and later progressed to BIM Modeler cum Drafter at King Construction. My work has involved construction drawing preparation, site coordination, architectural drafting, 3D building modelling and technical documentation.

My core technical expertise includes AutoCAD 2D drafting and Autodesk Revit BIM modelling, particularly in architectural and structural drawing development. I am detail-oriented and responsible, and committed to producing accurate technical drawings while continuously developing my BIM capabilities.
